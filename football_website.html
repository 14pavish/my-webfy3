Positions Explained

Goalkeeper (GK)
The only player who can use their hands. Guards the goal and organises the defence.
Defenders — LB, CB, RB
Protect the goal. Centre backs deal with strikers; full backs (LB/RB) guard the sides of the pitch.
Midfielders — LM, RM
The engine room. Win possession, distribute passes, support both attack and defence.
Attackers — LW, ST, RW
Score goals. Wingers (LW/RW) play wide and cross; the Striker (ST) finishes chances in front of goal.
